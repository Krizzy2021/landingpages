Risk Mitigation

Office-Based Security & Operational Controls

Reduces unmanaged offshore risk by keeping all production inside a controlled office facility with strict access, device, confidentiality, and supervision protocols.

Controlled Facilities

All work is performed exclusively from supervised facilities in Davao City — never home-based freelancer environments.

No Personal Devices

Personal cell phones are strictly prohibited on active production floors to safeguard confidential client data.

SOC 2 & Access Scope

Role-based access controls, password manager discipline, client-approved SSO/2FA, and SOC 2 aligned protocols.

Network Continuity
